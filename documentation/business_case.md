# Project Planning: E-Commerce Sales & Business Performance Analysis

## Scenario
I am acting as a Junior Business Analyst for an international E-commerce company that sells products across the global market. The Financial Department has requested valuable insights regarding sales performance, customers, products, markets, and overall profitability to support data-driven business decisions.

## Objective
The primary objective of this project is to use SQL to analyze complex commercial data. This analysis demonstrates practical skills in handling financial metrics, identifying critical sales and customer trends, evaluating product/market performance, and delivering actionable business recommendations.

## Business Questions
1. **Monthly Sales Performance:** How are total revenue, number of orders, and average order value changing over time?
2. **Top Customers & Profitability:** Who are the top 10 customers by revenue, and how profitable are they?
3. **Customer Segment Performance:** Which customer segments generate the most revenue and profit?
4. **Category & Sub-Category Profitability:** Which product categories and sub-categories are the most and least profitable?
5. **High-Sales, Low-Profit Products:** Which products generate high sales but poor or negative profitability?
6. **Country Revenue & Profit Contribution:** Which countries generate strong sales but weak profitability?
7. **Discount Impact Analysis:** How do different discount levels affect sales volume, revenue, and profit?
8. **Shipping Mode Efficiency:** Which shipping modes are the most financially efficient?
9. **Product Profit Ranking:** What are the top 3 most profitable products within each product category?
10. **Largest Loss-Making Transactions:** Which individual sales transactions generated the largest losses, and what do they have in common?
